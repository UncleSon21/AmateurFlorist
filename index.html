Questions

Before you order

Can I check my flowers before I pay?
Yes. Send an order request, and nothing is charged. We'll contact you to confirm it, and a $10 deposit by PayID secures your order. You pay the rest only once you've seen your bouquet and you're happy with it. If you're not, you don't have to buy it; we keep the $10 to cover making it. Change your mind before we start making it, and we'll refund the $10.
Do you have a physical store?
No. We're a small online florist based in Sydney, with no shopfront. Most orders are delivered. If you'd rather pick up, choose Pickup at checkout and we'll arrange a time and place with you.
Are your flowers fresh or artificial?
Both, and every bouquet is labelled. The Living Collection is fresh flowers. The Forever Collection is silk (artificial) and preserved flowers, made to last for years with no water.
Where do you deliver, and how much is it?
Across Greater Sydney (check your postcode). Delivery is free on orders over $50, otherwise $15. Order before 2pm for same-day delivery.
How do I order?
Add a bouquet to your cart and send an order request at checkout, message us on Instagram, or email chloe@amateurflorist.co.
Can you make something custom?
Yes. Message us with the occasion, your budget and when you need it, and we'll help you choose a bouquet or make one for you.
Do you do wedding flowers?
Yes: bouquets and ceremony pieces to hire or buy, or designed around your story. See wedding flowers.
