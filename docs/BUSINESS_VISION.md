# Floral Business Vision

> Reference document. `CLAUDE.md` summarises the parts that affect code;
> this is the full thinking behind it.

## Core Vision

Instead of becoming a traditional florist, build a **modern floral brand** that focuses on creating lasting memories rather than simply selling flowers.

**Brand mission:**

> *We help people celebrate life's moments with beautiful floral creations that last—through keepsake bouquets, flexible wedding rentals, and personalised floral experiences.*

The business isn't competing on "artificial flowers." It competes on **experience, personalization, convenience, and memories.**

---

## Target Markets

### Everyday Gifts

Products for birthdays, graduations, anniversaries, Mother's Day, Valentine's Day and housewarming gifts. Focus on products that customers can keep and display.

Examples: premium artificial bouquets, crochet bouquets, preserved flowers, satin ribbon bouquets, LEGO-inspired floral gifts, home décor arrangements.

### Wedding Collection

Offer both **purchase** and **rental** options.

Products: bridal bouquets, bridesmaid bouquets, groom boutonnières, parents' corsages, ceremony décor, reception centrepieces, floral arches.

Possible services: bouquet rental; buy instead of rent; rent now, purchase later; matching wedding collections; custom colour palettes.

Long-term idea: recreate a couple's fresh wedding bouquet using premium artificial flowers as a keepsake.

---

## Why Artificial Flowers?

Advantages: they last for years, suit home decoration, waste less inventory, can be ordered well in advance, are easier to customize, and are ideal for keepsakes.

Rather than marketing them as "artificial flowers", market them as keepsake bouquets, forever blooms, lasting bouquets, or botanical keepsakes.

Sell the **emotion**, not the material.

---

## Wedding Rental Business

A rental model similar to wedding dress hire.

Benefits: more affordable for couples, premium appearance, the bouquet is only needed for one day, and inventory can generate revenue multiple times.

Business considerations: security deposits, a clear damage policy, protective carry cases, easy repair and refurbishment, a buy-after-rental option, and avoiding the use of rental bouquets for bouquet tosses (offer a separate toss bouquet instead).

## Website Vision

**Shop** — browse by occasion, wedding theme, or collection (modern, garden, rustic, white & green, romantic, minimalist).

**Wedding Rentals** — real-time availability, wedding date calendar, online reservation, deposit payment, pickup and return scheduling, automatic reminders, and a buy-instead-of-returning option.

**Wedding Packages** — bundle the bridal bouquet, bridesmaids, boutonnières, centrepieces and ceremony décor. One click and everything matches.

**Custom Design Studio** — let customers customize flower types, colours, bouquet style, wrapping, ribbon, budget, add-ons and a personal message. Eventually include previews before ordering.

**Memory Bouquets** — customers provide a wedding invitation, favourite colours, photos, a personal story, or meaningful flowers, and receive a custom bouquet inspired by their memories. The business sells stories, not just flowers.

---

## Admin Dashboard

Build software that small florists usually don't have: inventory management, rental tracking, an order dashboard, a delivery calendar, a customer database, automatic invoices and email confirmations.

Future analytics: revenue, repeat customers, popular colours, best-selling bouquets, most rented products.

---

## Future Physical Store

Create an experience instead of a traditional flower shop: a build-your-own-bouquet bar where customers choose flowers, wrapping and ribbons and assemble bouquets with staff assistance.

Also offer bouquet-making workshops, couples' workshops, Mother's Day events, bridal shower workshops and team-building experiences.

The goal is for customers to visit for the experience, not just the products.

---

## Brand Identity

The business should feel premium and emotional.

Tagline ideas: Flowers That Become Memories · Beautiful Blooms. Lasting Moments. · Memories You Can Hold · Flowers Made to Last · Crafted for Every Moment

---

## Business Roadmap

**Phase 1 – Validate Demand.** Focus on everyday keepsake bouquets, wedding rentals and wedding purchases. Build around 20 excellent products instead of hundreds. Launch the website, social media, wedding packages and the rental system. The primary goal is to answer: *will customers actually pay for this?*

**Phase 2 – Grow.** Expand wedding collections, home décor, corporate flowers, workshops and the custom bouquet builder. Improve analytics, inventory, customer management and marketing.

**Phase 3 – Physical Store.** Create a destination where people buy gifts, rent wedding flowers, design their own bouquets, attend workshops and build lasting memories.

---

## Biggest Competitive Advantage

The website is **not** the competitive advantage. The business model is: keepsake bouquets, wedding bouquet rentals, buy-or-rent flexibility, memory bouquets, matching wedding collections, build-your-own bouquet experiences, and a modern online booking and management system.

Together these create a floral brand that is difficult to copy because it combines thoughtful products with a memorable customer experience.

## Open questions

Deliberately unresolved — flag rather than assume:

- No pricing, cost, margin or volume targets exist yet. "Will customers pay for
  this?" is not testable until it reads more like: *can we book 6 hires in the
  next 4 months at $400+ average, from a standing start?*
- Customer acquisition is unaddressed and is the genuinely hard problem.
  Weddings are discovered via Instagram, venues, planners and directories;
  gifting via Google and local SEO. These are different channels.
- Storage space for hire inventory is unaccounted for.
- Sydney weddings cluster into Sept–April Saturdays. Utilisation, not reuse,
  decides whether the hire model works — peak dates collide, so popular items
  need duplicates while sitting idle all winter.
- Bridal bouquet hire carries emotional friction (carrying a bouquet another
  bride carried; wanting to keep and preserve it). Ceremony décor — arches,
  centrepieces, plinths — has far less friction, higher ticket, and a proven
  Sydney hire market. It may be the better entry point.
- The daily work of a hire business is logistics — stock, storage, dispatch,
  returns, inspection, repair — not floristry. Worth confirming the owner wants
  that job before the model is built around it.
