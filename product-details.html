Care instructions

  1. Remove flowers from packaging immediately upon arrival
  2. Cut 2–3cm off stems at a 45° angle under running water
  3. Remove any leaves below the waterline
  4. Place in a clean vase with fresh, cool water
  5. Add the flower food sachet if included
  6. Change water every 2–3 days and re-trim stems
  7. Keep away from direct sunlight, heat vents, and fruit
