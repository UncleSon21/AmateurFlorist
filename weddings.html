Common questions

The things couples ask first

Can't find your question? Email us at weddings@vaniaflorist.com.au — we usually reply within a day.

How far in advance should we book?+

For Saturdays in peak season (Sep–Apr), we recommend 6–9 months ahead. Off-peak weekdays we can sometimes accommodate as little as 6 weeks. For destination/forever florals, the sooner the better — preserved orders take 4–8 weeks of lead time.

Do you mix fresh and forever in one wedding?+

All the time — it's actually one of our most-loved combinations. Fresh bouquet for the day, preserved replicas for the keepsakes. Or fresh ceremony flowers with reusable artificial centrepieces (much friendlier on the budget too).

What if our flower preferences are out of season?+

We'll always tell you honestly. Fresh peonies in July, for example, are imported and cost 3× — we'll suggest gorgeous in-season alternatives, or recommend our preserved range for that exact look without the seasonal price.

Do you travel outside Sydney?+

Yes — we regularly serve Blue Mountains, Southern Highlands, Central Coast, and Hunter Valley. Travel is quoted at cost. For destination weddings interstate, our forever range ships nationally and we can fly out for setup on bespoke packages.

What's your deposit and cancellation policy?+

30% deposit secures your date. Cancellations more than 60 days out receive 80% refund of paid amount; 30–60 days, 50%; under 30 days, deposits are non-refundable but can transfer to a future date within 12 months.

Can we see and smell flowers before deciding?+

Absolutely — our Surry Hills studio is open by appointment. We'll have seasonal samples ready, and on bespoke packages we offer a paid mock-up bouquet (cost rolled into final invoice) so you can see your exact arrangement before the day.
