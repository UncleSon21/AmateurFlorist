Common questions

The things couples ask first

Can't find your question? Ask it in the form below, or email us at hello@amateurflorist.co.

How far in advance should we book?+

As early as you can — Saturdays from September to April go first. Hire bookings need to be made at least two weeks before the day. Enquiring doesn't hold a bouquet; confirming your booking does.

Do you mix fresh and forever in one wedding?+

Yes — for example, a fresh bouquet for the day with silk pieces you keep, or fresh ceremony flowers with reusable silk centrepieces.

What if our flower preferences are out of season?+

We'll always tell you honestly what's in season and suggest alternatives — or a silk version of the look you want, which doesn't depend on the season at all.
